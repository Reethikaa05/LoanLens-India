# Case 1: Priya (29, Bengaluru · Salaried MNC Tech)

### Profile Summary
- **Employment:** Software Engineer at a large Tier-A MNC for 5 years.
- **Monthly Inflow:** Net ₹1,10,000/month.
- **Existing Debt:** One car loan (EMI ₹14,000, 2 years left).
- **Credit Score:** 780 CIBIL (Top 10% prime credit tier).
- **Living Costs:** Rents in Bengaluru at ₹28,000; total living essentials ~₹48,000/month.
- **The Ask:** Wants ₹8,00,000 personal loan for a wedding.

### Questions Asked (Adaptive Questionnaire)
1. *Loan Purpose:* Wedding / Family Function (Discretionary Consumption)
2. *Requested Amount:* ₹8,00,000 (Tenure: 48 Months)
3. *Employment Type:* Salaried MNC Corporate
4. *Net Take-Home Monthly Inflow:* ₹1,10,000
5. *Essential Living Expenses (Rent + Food):* ₹48,000
6. *Existing Monthly EMIs:* ₹14,000
7. *Credit Score Band:* 780+ (Excellent Prime)
8. *Age:* 29
9. *Collateral Available:* None (Unsecured personal loan)
10. *Recent Bounces (12m):* Zero

### The Four Outputs (O1 – O4)
- **O1: Verdict → `BORROW_LESS` (Downsize to ₹4.95 Lakhs)**
  - *Headline:* Borrow Less: Cap at Safe Discretionary Limit.
  - *Rationale:* Taking ₹8.0 Lakhs personal loan for a non-yielding wedding while servicing an active car EMI commits ₹22,000/month for 4 years with over ₹1.9 Lakhs in interest drag.
  - *Recommended Product:* Pruned Personal Loan with bonus prepayment plan.
- **O2: Maximum Amount → Lender Sanction vs Safe Carry**
  - *Lender Sanction (60% FOIR):* **₹20.31 Lakhs** (Lenders will happily sanction up to ₹20.3L because they ignore rent & lifestyle).
  - *Borrower Safe Carry:* **₹4.95 Lakhs** (Capped at 4.5x net monthly income for non-yielding consumption).
  - *Delta:* ₹15.36 Lakhs gap. The app explicitly instructs: *"Use the Safe Carry number (₹4.95L) — the lender does not pay your Bangalore rent or replenish your savings."*
- **O3: Fair Interest Rate & True APR**
  - *Fair Rate Band:* **10.50% – 11.75% p.a.** (Benchmark: Repo 6.50% + 4.00% to 5.25% prime spread).
  - *All-In APR (with 1% fee + 18% GST):* **10.94% – 12.19% p.a.**
  - *Processing Fee Ceiling:* Cap at 0.50% (Max ₹2,500 – ₹4,000).
- **O4: Monthly EMI Ceiling & Stress Testing**
  - *Safe Monthly Ceiling:* **₹26,000/month**.
  - *Recommended EMI (₹4.95L at 10.5% for 48m):* **₹12,684/month**.
  - *Stress Test 1 (20% Income Slump):* Survives with ₹6,316/month surplus buffer remaining.
  - *Stress Test 2 (+200 bps Rate Spike):* Monthly EMI increases by +₹492/mo, safely absorbed by monthly cushion.
  - *Stress Test 3 (₹40,000 Emergency Hospitalization):* 4 months emergency savings absorb the shock without debt.

# Case 2: Ravi (42, Mysuru · Self-Employed Kirana Store)

### Profile Summary
- **Employment:** Kirana Store Owner for 14 years.
- **Monthly Inflow:** Cash income ₹40,000 – ₹80,000/month (midpoint ₹60,000); ITR declares ₹4,20,000/year (₹35,000/month declared).
- **Key Asset:** Owns the commercial shop premises in Mysuru, about **₹45,00,000**, unencumbered!
- **Credit Score:** None / Unknown (Never taken a formal bank loan).
- **Spouse:** Wife earns ₹18,000/month teaching (stable banking salary credit).
- **Existing Debt:** Zero.
- **Living Costs:** ₹32,000/month.
- **The Ask:** Wants ₹15,00,000 for a second stock line and a delivery vehicle.

### Questions Asked (Adaptive Questionnaire)
1. *Loan Purpose:* Business Growth / Stock & Vehicle
2. *Requested Amount:* ₹15,00,000 (Tenure: 84 Months / 7 Years)
3. *Employment Type:* Self-Employed Business
4. *Net Inflow:* ₹60,000 (Cash + Declared)
5. *Annual ITR Declared:* ₹4,20,000 (Triggers 50% haircut rule on cash difference)
6. *Collateral Available:* Commercial Property valued at ₹45,00,000
7. *Co-Applicant:* Yes, Wife earning ₹18,000/month (Teacher)
8. *Credit Score:* Unknown (Never borrowed formally)
9. *Existing EMIs:* 0

### The Four Outputs (O1 – O4)
- **O1: Verdict → `BORROW` (Via Secured LAP, NOT Unsecured MSME)**
  - *Headline:* Borrow via Secured LAP, NOT Unsecured Business Loan.
  - *Rationale:* Unsecured MSME lenders will exploit Ravi's lack of credit score and high cash share by quoting 18%–22%. Pledging the ₹45L unencumbered shop unlocks a commercial LAP at 9.25%–11.25%, saving **₹8.4 Lakhs in interest** over 7 years!
  - *Recommended Product:* Commercial Loan Against Property (SME Micro-LAP) with Wife as Co-applicant.
- **O2: Maximum Amount → Lender Sanction vs Safe Carry**
  - *Lender Sanction (Secured LAP at 50% Commercial LTV):* **₹22.50 Lakhs**.
  - *Borrower Safe Carry:* **₹16.80 Lakhs** (Anchored by ₹30,400 safe monthly cashflow + ₹15k incremental stock profits).
  - *Verdict on ₹15L Ask:* Fully affordable when structured as 7-year LAP.
- **O3: Fair Interest Rate & True APR**
  - *Fair Rate Band:* **9.25% – 11.25% p.a.** (Benchmark: Repo 6.50% + 2.75% to 4.75% secured spread).
  - *All-In APR:* **9.49% – 11.49% p.a.** (0.75% processing fee).
  - *Unsecured Avoidance Value:* Saves ~8.50% per annum compared to 19.5% unsecured cards.
- **O4: Monthly EMI Ceiling & Stress Testing**
  - *Safe Monthly Ceiling:* **₹30,400/month**.
  - *Recommended EMI (₹15L at 9.75% for 84m):* **₹24,712/month**.
  - *Stress Test 1 (20% Business Income Drop):* Survives with ₹9,088/month buffer because spouse's teaching salary (₹18,000) provides an unbreakable floor.
  - *Stress Test 2 (+200 bps Floating Spike):* Monthly EMI increases to ₹26,245/mo, still comfortably inside the ₹30,400 ceiling.

# Case 3: Anita (35, Hubballi · Informal Gig Worker)

### Profile Summary
- **Employment:** Delivery-platform rider (Zomato/Swiggy) plus home tailoring.
- **Monthly Inflow:** ₹26,000 – ₹30,000/month (midpoint ₹28,000).
- **Household Context:** Two children; husband unemployed for 8 months.
- **Existing Debt:** Three instant app loans totaling **₹35,000 at 30%+ interest**, with **one EMI bounced last month**. Current monthly EMIs are ~₹7,500.
- **Living Costs:** ₹22,000/month (food, children's school fees, kerosene/LPG).
- **The Ask:** Wants ₹1,50,000 for an electric scooter to double delivery runs.

### Questions Asked (Adaptive Questionnaire)
1. *Loan Purpose:* Two-Wheeler Asset (EV Scooter for delivery work)
2. *Requested Amount:* ₹1,50,000 (Tenure: 24 Months)
3. *Employment Type:* Informal / Gig Worker
4. *Net Inflow:* ₹28,000
5. *Essential Living Expenses:* ₹22,000
6. *Existing Monthly EMIs:* ₹7,500 (3 app loans at 30%+)
7. *Recent Bounces (12m):* Yes (1 bounce last month)
8. *Highest Existing Rate:* 34.0% (Predatory instant fintech apps)
9. *Expected Earnings from EV:* ₹15,000/month (Doubling delivery runs)
10. *Emergency Savings:* Under 1 month (₹5,000)

### The Four Outputs (O1 – O4)
- **O1: Verdict → `DONT_BORROW` (Refuse Unsecured Personal Loans)**
  - *Headline:* Do Not Take Another Unsecured Loan.
  - *Rationale:* Anita is in an active predatory debt spiral: paying ₹7,500/month on 30%+ app loans with a recent bounce. Taking another ₹1.5L unsecured loan from a fintech app at 32% will cause total household insolvency.
  - *Recommended Product:* Mudra Shishu EV Asset Loan (<11%) with vehicle hypothecation, combined with immediate settlement of existing app loans.
  - *Action Steps:* Settle app loans first; do NOT apply for personal loans; apply through EV dealership tie-up under government priority sector schemes.
- **O2: Maximum Amount → Lender Sanction vs Safe Carry**
  - *Lender Sanction (App lenders):* Might sanction ₹1.2L–₹1.5L at extortionate 36% APR.
  - *Borrower Safe Carry (Unsecured):* **₹0** (Anita currently has **-₹1,500/month deficit** before adding new debt).
  - *Asset-Backed Carry (With EV Earnings):* ₹1.45 Lakhs if financed via subsidized EV priority credit where the scooter itself is the collateral.
- **O3: Fair Interest Rate & True APR**
  - *Unsecured App Rate:* 30.00% – 36.00% (Predatory danger zone).
  - *Fair Priority Sector EV Band:* **9.50% – 11.50% p.a.** under Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) / EV Priority Lending.
- **O4: Monthly EMI Ceiling & Stress Testing**
  - *Safe Monthly Ceiling:* **₹4,200/month** (Only after consolidating existing app debt).
  - *Stress Test 1 (20% Income Drop):* **FAILS** (Household falls into -₹6,800/mo deficit).
  - *Stress Test 3 (₹40,000 Health Shock):* **FAILS** (Emergency fund is depleted; immediate risk of debt bondage without MFI relief).
